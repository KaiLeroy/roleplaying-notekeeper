Place · District

The Undermarket

Kestrel Landing neutral ground visited

Three levels of flooded cistern under Kestrel Landing, drained by the Tidewright Union and never re-plumbed. Stalls run on the second gallery; the third is water again by midwinter.

No faction holds it outright. House Calder keeps two collectors at the stair, and everyone pretends not to see them. Sirin Vale runs messages through the fish gallery on nights the tide is high — she took Verrin's letter on night three without asking who it was for.

Standing rule of the place, repeated at the stair by whoever is collecting: no blades drawn below the waterline. Enforced by the crowd rather than by anyone in particular.
